# 📊 Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard

### Power BI | Data Analysis | Insurance Analytics | Business Intelligence

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze insurance policies, claims, customer demographics, vehicle characteristics, coverage zones, and household driving behavior.

The dashboard provides two analytical views using a dynamic measure selector:

- **Total Claim Amount**
- **Total Policies**

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform insurance data into an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders understand:

- Insurance policy distribution
- Total claim exposure
- Claim frequency and average claim amount
- Customer demographics
- Vehicle-related patterns
- Geographic coverage
- Household driving behavior

The dashboard was developed based on defined business requirements for insurance risk and claims analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard analyzes insurance data across multiple dimensions:

| Analysis | Visualization |
|---|---|
| Car Use | Donut Chart |
| Car Make | Bar Chart |
| Coverage Zone | Donut Chart |
| Age Group | Column Chart |
| Car Year | Area Chart |
| Kids Driving | Area / Ribbon Chart |
| Education | Pie Chart |
| Education & Marital Status | Matrix |

### Key analytical areas

- **Car Use** – Compare Personal and Commercial vehicle policies/claims
- **Car Make** – Identify policy and claim concentration by manufacturer
- **Coverage Zone** – Analyze geographic policy and claim distribution
- **Age Group** – Compare policies and claims across customer age groups
- **Car Year** – Analyze policy and claim trends by vehicle year
- **Kids Driving** – Explore household driving patterns
- **Education** – Compare policy and claim distribution by education
- **Education & Marital Status** – Analyze combined customer segments

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

### Dynamic Measure Selection

The **Select Measure** dropdown allows users to switch between:

```text
Total Claim Amount
        ↕
Total Policies
```

This makes it possible to analyze the same dimensions from both a **claim exposure** and **policy distribution** perspective.
